FIIB has been shaping future-ready business leaders since 1995, with a legacy rooted in experiential learning, innovation and real-world business thinking. This belief gave rise to Meraki in 2012 — a platform to get challenged, meet experts, make connections and win recognition. It brings together ambitious students, mentors, investors and industry leaders to challenge ideas and turn potential into action.

Meraki 2026 is a three-phase entrepreneurship pitch competition for undergraduate and postgraduate students, featuring SDG/Social Impact, Women Founders, and AI/Tech tracks. With ₹5 lakh+ in prizes, mentorship and incubation opportunities, Meraki is where ideas get tested, sharpened and taken further.
